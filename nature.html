Principles for responsible technology

Make considered choices. Be clear about evidence.

Digital services depend on devices, networks and energy. We consider these costs while designing useful services and information for people.

⌁
Thoughtful design

Choose the simplest useful approach

Prioritise clear pages and systems that are practical to maintain. A well-scoped solution is easier to operate and improve over time.

↻
Care for devices

Keep devices useful for longer

When a project allows, consider maintenance, repair, reuse and responsible recycling before replacing devices.

How we will stay honest

Intentions, with accountability.

Environmental responsibility takes ongoing work. We will keep our claims proportionate to the evidence and actions we can show.

  • Prefer specific, explainable actions over broad “green” claims.
  • Do not publish carbon savings, biodiversity gains or impact totals without a reliable basis.
  • Consider accessibility, data minimisation, device life and maintainability when shaping digital projects.
  • Listen to local organisations and experts before representing community or conservation work.
  • Review this page as our actions and evidence develop.
