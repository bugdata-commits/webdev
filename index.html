Hosting, your way

Get online. Choose the right foundation.

Not every website needs the same infrastructure. Bugdata offers a simple starting point for eligible static sites and managed hosting for projects that need more.

A simple way to start

Free-for-life static hosting

$0 hosting fee

For eligible static websites, Bugdata can deploy and maintain the site through GitHub Pages, with no hosting charge from Bugdata.

  • Suitable for static business websites
  • Git-based deployment workflow
  • HTTPS support on supported configurations
  • Custom domain connection where eligible
  • Upgrade path when your needs grow

GitHub Pages has platform terms, usage limits and eligibility rules. Domain registration, advanced support, dynamic backends and third-party services may cost extra. “Free-for-life” describes Bugdata's hosting fee for qualifying sites, while the platform remains subject to GitHub's terms.

A few useful answers

Questions, answered.

Can Bugdata build a website from my own idea or design?

Yes. We can work from a brief, reference website, existing brand assets or a custom design direction. We'll confirm feasibility and scope before starting.

What does free-for-life hosting mean?

Bugdata can host eligible static websites through GitHub Pages without charging a hosting fee. GitHub's platform terms and usage limits apply. Domains, dynamic backends, advanced support and third-party services may have separate costs.

When should I choose managed hosting?

Managed hosting is a better fit when your project needs server-side code, databases, content management, application services or resources beyond static hosting. The exact fit depends on the technology and expected usage.

Can you integrate WhatsApp, AI and booking tools?

We can scope integrations with supported platforms and APIs. Availability, subscription fees, account approvals and third-party usage charges depend on the chosen services.

Do you work with businesses outside Zimbabwe?

Yes. Bugdata can discuss and deliver suitable digital projects remotely. Availability and project arrangements are confirmed during discovery.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on functionality, content, integrations, hosting and delivery requirements. Send us your brief and we'll discuss a suitable scope and proposal.
