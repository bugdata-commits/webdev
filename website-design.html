Website offer FAQs.

What does the $24.99 Zimbabwean-domain offer include?

Website design for one business website, registration of one eligible Zimbabwean domain for the first year, foundational SEO, HTTPS/SSL where supported, and your choice of the included hosting offer: one year of free website hosting or eligible static hosting through GitHub Pages with no Bugdata hosting fee for life. Scope and domain availability are confirmed before work begins.

What does the $50 .com offer include?

It includes website design, one .com domain registration for the first year, foundational SEO, HTTPS/SSL where supported and the same choice of hosting offer. Domain renewals after year one are not included.

What counts as foundational SEO?

It is the initial on-page and technical setup described above. Ongoing SEO campaigns, regular content creation, paid search advertising, ranking positions and traffic are not guaranteed and are not included unless separately agreed.

Can any website use free-for-life GitHub Pages hosting?

No. GitHub Pages is suitable for eligible static websites. Sites requiring databases, server-side features or application backends will need a different hosting arrangement. GitHub’s terms and limits apply.

Are domain renewals included?

The offer includes first-year domain registration for the selected eligible domain. Renewal fees from year two onward are separate and depend on the domain extension and registrar at renewal time.

Will my site be secure?

We’ll configure HTTPS/SSL where supported by the chosen hosting platform and domain setup. HTTPS protects data in transit; it does not by itself guarantee protection from every security threat.

How do I get a quote for extra pages or features?

Message Bugdata with your desired pages, content and features. We’ll confirm what fits the promotion and quote any additional work before you commit.
